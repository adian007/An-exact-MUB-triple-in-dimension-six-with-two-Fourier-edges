Abstract
The complete classiﬁcation of 6 ×6 complex Hadamard matrices (CHMs) is a long-standing
open problem. In this paper we investigate a series of CHMs, s uch as the CHMs containing a 2 ×3
submatrix with rank one, the CHMs containing exactly three d istinct elements and all elements of
theﬁrstrowbeingone, the H2-reduciblematrices containing exactly threedistinctmat rix elements,
and the CHMs containing a 3 ×3 Hadamard submatrix. We characterize all forms of these CHM s.
Our results are the latest progress on the complete classiﬁc ation of CHMs.

I. INTRODUCTION
For describing observables in quantum physics, Schwinger deﬁned t he MUBs [ 1] in 1960.
It is known that two quantum states in Cdare mutually unbiased (MU) when their inner
product has modulus1√
d. Two orthogonal bases in Cdare MU when their elements are
all MU. We refer to the complete MUBs as d+ 1 orthogonal bases in Cdand any two of
them are MU. Ref.[ 2] found that the complete MUBs in dimension dexist ifdis a prime
power. So the ﬁrst unknown case occurs at d= 6. People have studied this case by many
methods, such asthe average distance between four bases in dime nsion six [ 3], Fourier family
of Hadamard matrices [ 4], computer program [ 5], noise robustness [ 6], product basis [ 7–10]
and so on [ 11–17]. This case is also a long-standing open problem.
We refer to the complex Hadamard matrix (CHM) H as an n×nmatrix with elements of
modulus one, and1√nH is a unitary matrix. For convenience, we ignore the coeﬃcient1√n
of the CHM when we discuss four mutually unbiased bases (MUBs) con taining the identity
matrix in the following content. In this paper we investigate some 6 ×6 CHMs. We prove
that allH2-reducible matrices deﬁned by Karlsson in [ 18] containing exactly three distinct
matrix elements are complex equivalent to the speciﬁc matrix M2in (14). This is presented
in Lemma 8. We ﬁnd a new family of CHMs and we obtain that all 6 ×6 CHMs containing
a 3×3 Hadamard submatrix are complex equivalent to the matrices from t he new CHM
family. They are the latest results on 6 ×6 CHMs and they might lead to the complete
classiﬁcation of 6 ×6 CHMs. TO BE CONTINUED .

Tao matrix. We believe more non- H2-reducible matrices will lead to the solution of the
famous MUB problem in dimension six.
