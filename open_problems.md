# Genuine Open Problems

1. Determine the exact value of `N(6)`.
2. Prove or disprove existence of four MUBs in `C^6`.
3. Independently reproduce or referee the Wuttig--Tindall v2 complete CHM classification and its Lean/exact certificates.
4. Prove fourth-MUB nonexistence on the full Karlsson Dita circle, not merely at selected points or samples.
5. Prove fourth-MUB nonexistence on all of `K_6^(3)`.
6. Establish an exact, complete-pool description of the third-MUB loci in the Karlsson parameter space.
7. Produce an exact algebraic third basis and a unit-ideal `W1` certificate at one algebraic Dita/Karlsson point not equivalent to `D0`.
8. Clarify the exact transition parameters where the Diţă MU-vector constellation changes from 120 to 72 or 48 vectors, with proofs rather than sampled thresholds.
9. Reconcile all later exclusion claims based on the Chen et al. lemma after the McNulty--Weigert 2025 correction.
10. Verify the original Zauner thesis directly before assigning page- or theorem-level citations to the MUB conjecture.

Not open: the D0 nonextendability statement itself; Brierley--Weigert already provide that result. Also not open as a repository implementation question: the Liang/Chen route has no verified Karlsson-style continuous family in the cited sources.
