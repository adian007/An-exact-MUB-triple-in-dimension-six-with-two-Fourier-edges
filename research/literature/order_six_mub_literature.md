# Literature review: order six MUBs and Hadamards

## Primary sources

### Bengtsson et al. (2007)

*MUBs and Hadamards of Order Six*, arXiv:quant-ph/0610161, J. Math. Phys. 48, 052106 (2007).

The paper treats the landscape of order-six complex Hadamards as the natural search space for MUB triplets, introduces a distance viewpoint, and reports triplets rather than sets near the prime-power upper bound. It is especially relevant to the project's locus viewpoint: a third-MUB condition is expected to occupy a thin subset of Hadamard parameter space.

### Grassl (2004)

*On SIC-POVMs and MUBs in Dimension 6*, arXiv:quant-ph/0406175.

The abstract reports several maximal sets of three MUBs in dimension six. This is directly relevant to the Fourier anchor: a known triple can be maximal, so failure to find a fourth basis is not surprising at special anchors. The result is not a theorem about every CHM or every triple.

### Brierley and Weigert (2009)

*Constructing Mutually Unbiased Bases in Dimension Six*, arXiv:0901.4051, Phys. Rev. A 79, 052316 (2009).

They prescribe a first Hadamard, construct all other MU Hadamards by algebraic computation, sample known families, and report no more than two additional MU Hadamards in the tested setting. The local project reproduces the important Dita/D0 counts and uses a different one-vector witness formulation for selected obstructions.

## Relevance to the current problem

The literature supports three statements:

1. dimension six is exceptional because it is composite and no complete `d+1` construction is known;
2. maximal triples exist and are structurally tied to special CHM loci;
3. exhaustive fixed-matrix algebra is more credible than a generic floating-point sweep.

It does not supply a family-wide proof that every `K_6^(3)` triple is maximal. That remains the project's open mathematical target.

## Citation hygiene

Use the exact arXiv version in claims. Do not conflate Brierley--Weigert 2008's numerical constellation paper with the 2009 algebraic construction paper. Treat McNulty--Weigert's printed Karlsson transcription as a separate object until the local CHM audit passes.
