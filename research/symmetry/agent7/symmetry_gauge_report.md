# Agent 7: exact symmetry and gauge audit

## Scope and verdict

This is a no-sweep audit.  It separates transformations proved to preserve a
MUB configuration from transformations that merely produce another CHM.  The
relevant configuration is an ordered matrix representative
`(I,H,B3)` with normalized bases `I`, `H/sqrt(6)`, and `B3/sqrt(6)`; `B3` is
only admissible after the required basis-level tests.  Define

`W1(H,B3) = {v : v is MU to I, H/sqrt(6), and B3/sqrt(6)}`.

The exact quotient acting on MUB *sets* is generated by (i) a common unitary
on all bases, (ii) independent monomial transformations on the right of each
basis matrix, (iii) permutations of basis labels, and (iv) simultaneous
complex conjugation.  After fixing the computational basis, the common
unitary may be restricted to a left monomial transformation.  Every such
action induces a bijection on `W1`; therefore emptiness, existence of a
fourth basis, and the universal quantifier over **all** third bases transfer.

`H -> H^T` is **not** included.  Although `H^T` is a CHM, transposing a
candidate third basis does not in general preserve its cross overlaps with
`H^T`.  A transpose may only be used where an additional explicit monomial or
antiunitary equivalence witness has been proved.  The repository's residual
script tries transpose variants as a *search aid*; that is not an exact
transfer theorem.

The row-by-row catalogue is `transformation_catalogue.csv`.

## Exact equations and transfer lemma

For normalized basis matrices `U_a`, MUB means

`U_a^* U_a = I`, and `|(U_a^* U_b)_{jk}|^2=1/6` for `a != b`.

For unnormalized CHMs the equivalent tests are `M M^dagger=6I`,
`N N^dagger=6I`, and `|(M^dagger N)_{jk}|^2=1`.  If `L` is unitary and each
`R_a` is monomial, then

`(L U_a R_a)^* (L U_b R_b) = R_a^* (U_a^* U_b) R_b`.

Both sides preserve entry moduli and orthonormality.  If `L` is monomial,
`L I` is the computational basis as an unordered collection of rays.  Hence

`v in W1(H,B3) <=> L v in W1(L H R_H, L B3 R_3)`.

Column phases/permutations of `H` and `B3` do not change their ray bases at
all.  Complex conjugation is antiunitary and gives
`|<conj(x),conj(y)>|=|<x,y>|`, hence `v -> conj(v)` is likewise a `W1`
bijection.  These statements are exact and cover a whole pool: a bijection
maps every admissible third basis, not just a selected clique.

## Karlsson-specific reductions

Karlsson's original construction is the appropriate primary source, not a
numerical residual.  It defines standard CHM equivalence as
`H2=D2 P2 H1 P1 D1` (Eq. 2.2); proves that all 16 sign choices of the four
`z_i` produce equivalent sets (Proposition 8); and proves that `Lambda ->
-Lambda` is redundant, allowing `theta,phi in [0,pi)` (Proposition 9).
Its one free `z1=exp(i psi1)` can be taken with `psi1 in [0,pi)` after the
sign identification.  The construction has Möbius-degenerate strata; the
paper explicitly warns that direct limiting/directional treatment is needed.

The repository independently has a stronger local identity at the seam:
substituting `theta=0` into the audited original A-block eliminates `phi`, so
`H(0,phi,lambda)` is constant in `phi` for its consistent seam builder.  This
is an exact parametrization redundancy **on the seam**, not a license to
identify off-seam points or to discard limit directions.  The exact
singularity equations from Karlsson are

`sin(theta)(sin(phi)-sqrt(3) cos(theta) cos(phi))=0` for `M_A`, and
`sin(theta)(sin(phi)+sqrt(3) cos(theta) cos(phi))=0` for `M_B`.

Both maps are degenerate at `theta=0` and at `(theta,phi)=(pi/2,0)` modulo
the original coordinate ranges.  These must be treated as strata, never
removed by division or by generic-fiber transfer.

Repository T1 gives exact `2pi` periodicity in lambda.  Its claimed
pi-periodicity of CHM class on the Diţă slice is presently supported by a
finite numerical monomial-alignment search at selected points; it is not an
exact formula valid on the entire circle in the retained artifacts.  The
source-level sign argument makes a pi-sized *Karlsson coordinate chart*
plausible, but all code/export branches should retain a verifiable induced
row/column monomial transform before using this reduction in a proof.

## Recommended domains and workflow

1. Use Karlsson's literature fundamental chart
   `theta,phi,psi1 in [0,pi)` only at the mathematical construction layer,
   together with the explicit sign/permutation bookkeeping required by
   Proposition 8.
2. For the current seam-resolved numerical builder, use
   `theta,phi in [0,pi)`, `lambda in [0,2pi)` as the conservative execution
   domain; quotient only exact identity `lambda mod 2pi` automatically.
3. Collapse the seam `theta=0` to `phi=0`, but retain it as a separate
   stratum and preserve all directional-limit analyses.
4. Partition the remaining domain by the two displayed Möbius degeneracy
   equations before continuation, saturation, or interval covering.  A
   certificate on the generic stratum does not automatically cover them.
5. For a proposed selected triple, canonicalize using left/right monomials,
   basis relabelling, and conjugation only after storing the transformation
   matrices and independently rechecking the full unnormalized basis tests.

## Repository audit and limitations

Audited files: `paper/main_theorems.tex`, `paper/methods_audit.tex`,
`src/Karlsson.jl`, `scripts/julia/gauge_equivalence_analysis.jl`, and
`scripts/python/chm_equivalence.py`, plus existing residual logs.  The latter
enumerates column permutations and phase fits and tries H/transpose/conjugate
variants.  A small residual is evidence for a candidate equivalence only.  It
is not a proof unless it is converted to exact matrices satisfying the stated
identity (or a certified enclosure establishes it).  In particular, do not
use the reported finite set of Diţă residuals to identify an entire orbit,
to cover exceptional fibres, or to transfer a certificate with no recorded
map.

No statement here identifies all Karlsson equivalences, all Diţă-slice
classes, all third bases, all CHM sectors, or a fourth-MUB answer in dimension
six.
