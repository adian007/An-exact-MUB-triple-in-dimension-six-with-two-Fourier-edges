# Anchor selection for further research

## Tier A: exact algebraic anchors

- D0 and the D0-equivalent pair used in the local Groebner calculation.
- Fourier-type matrices with roots of unity.
- Tao S6 where an exact transcription is available.

These anchors support exact coefficient generation, regression tests, and field audits.

## Tier B: certified Dita representatives

Use the seven local lambda representatives already certified, but treat CHM-equivalent values as a single structural class only after the equivalence map is explicitly recorded. The local referee log warns that some proposed representatives are equivalent under transpose/conjugation/permutation variants, so raw lambda labels must not be used as an independence argument.

## Tier C: boundary and singular probes

Probe the Dita constellation-change windows, the F6 arc endpoints, and Mobius denominator/pole candidates. These are mathematically valuable because failure modes and positive-dimensional components are most likely there.

## Selection rule

An anchor is high-value when it has:

- an exact parameter description;
- a reproducible pool count;
- at least one independently verified third basis;
- a known equivalence relation to other anchors;
- a manageable W1 mixed volume.

The next best experiment is therefore not another random interior point. It is a paired study of one generic Dita point and one nearby constellation-change point, with exact parameter arithmetic where possible.
