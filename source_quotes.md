# Source Quotes

Short quotations are kept to the wording exposed in the inspected arXiv HTML. PDF page numbers are marked `UNVERIFIED` where the PDF itself was not independently paginated in this pass.

## Bengtsson et al. (2007)

Source: [Mubs and Hadamards of Order Six](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0610161), HTML [v3](https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0610161v3). PDF page: UNVERIFIED.

- Sec. 1: “For N=6, all that is known is that the number of MUBs is at least 3 and at most 7.”
- Sec. 1: “We have carried through this strategy only piecemeal—and for N=6 we did not find any MUB quartets.”
- Sec. 2: “Two Hadamard matrices H1 and H2 are called equivalent ... if there exist diagonal unitary matrices ... and permutation matrices ...”
- Sec. 9: “A complete classification of the equivalence classes of N=6 complex Hadamard matrices ... is however not available.”
- Sec. 7, Eq. (78): “An explicit example of a MUB triplet including b2 is (1l, F_D, D_bc).”
- Sec. 7, Eqs. (79)-(80): `D_bc` is the block-circulant representative built from `C3` and `C4`.

## Grassl (2004)

Source: [On SIC-POVMs and MUBs in Dimension 6](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0406175), HTML [v2](https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0406175v2). PDF page: UNVERIFIED.

- Sec. 4: “As far as we know, it is still open what the maximal size N(6) of a set of mutually unbiased bases in dimension six is. Zauner conjectured that N(6)=3.”
- Theorem 2: “There are exactly 48 normalized vectors ...” for the Heisenberg pair.
- Theorem 2: “From the 48 vectors one can construct 16 different orthonormal bases ... All MUB_i are maximal in the sense that there is no vector ... unbiased with respect to all three bases.”

## Brierley--Weigert (2009)

Source: [Constructing Mutually Unbiased Bases in Dimension Six](https://arxiv.org/abs/0901.4051), HTML [v2](https://arxiv.org/html/0901.4051v2). PDF page: UNVERIFIED.

- Abstract: “we never find more than two that are mutually unbiased.” The abstract identifies this as support for the conjecture, not a proof.
- Sec. 2, Eq. (4): equivalent Hadamards are related by monomial row and column operations.
- Sec. 3.3: “there are 48 vectors that satisfy the Eqs. (16).”
- Sec. 3.3: “no two of these bases are MU with respect to each other.”
- Sec. 4.1: “A similar analysis for the Diţă matrix D0 reveals that there are 120 vectors ... 60 of which form ten bases but none of these are MU with respect to each other.”
- Sec. 4.1: “sets of four MU bases which include D0 do not exist.”
- Sec. 4.1, Eq. (20): the D0 phase set is `phi_D={0,pi, plus/minus k pi/12, plus/minus alpha}`, with `tan alpha=2`.
- Sec. 5: “The jury is still out” on the maximal number of MUBs in dimension six.

## Karlsson (2011)

Source: [Three-parameter complex Hadamard matrices of order 6](https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.4177), HTML [v1](https://arxiv.org/html/1003.4177v1). PDF page: UNVERIFIED.

- Abstract: “A three-parameter family of complex Hadamard matrices of order 6 is presented.”
- Theorem 11: “Any H2-reducible (complex) Hadamard matrices (of order 6) is equivalent to a member of the three-parameter family...”
- Sec. 7: “the characterization problem for H2-reducible Hadamard matrices has been fully described ...” and the full characterization remains partial/open.

## Maxwell--Brierley (2015)

Source: [On properties of Karlsson Hadamards and sets of Mutually Unbiased Bases in dimension six](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.4070), HTML [v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1402.4070v2). PDF page: UNVERIFIED.

- Abstract: “The complete classification of all 6x6 complex Hadamard matrices is an open problem.”
- Abstract: “Unfortunately running the linear programs we were unable to conclude that a complete set of mutually unbiased bases cannot be constructed from Karlsson Hadamards alone.”
- Introduction: Karlsson “does not fully classify all 6x6 Hadamard matrices” and excludes the isolated Spectral matrix.
- Theorem 4.1: for Karlsson matrices, `g_j(rho)=0` for permutations of `(1,1,1,-1,-1,-1)`.

## McNulty--Weigert (2025 correction)

Source: [Comment on 'Product states and Schmidt rank of mutually unbiased bases in dimension six'](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13067), HTML [v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.13067v1). PDF page: UNVERIFIED.

- Abstract: “The proof of the lemma is shown to contain a mistake, ultimately invalidating three theorems derived in later publications.”

## Wuttig--Tindall (2026 preprint)

Source: [A Complete Classification of Complex Hadamard Matrices of Order Six](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18053), HTML [v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.18053v2). PDF page: UNVERIFIED. This is a new preprint claim and has not been independently reproduced in this repository.

- Abstract: “Here, we give a complete and exact finite-incidence classification of order-six complex Hadamard matrices up to standard equivalence.”
- Theorem 6: “Every order-six complex Hadamard matrix is equivalent to a dephased matrix having a finite-corner witness.”
- Corollary 25: “The three historical sectors form an exhaustive list: H6=G6^(4) union K6^(3) union T6.”
- Outlook: “The classification does not settle the problem of mutually unbiased bases in C6.”
