# Current literature status relevant to the dimension-6 MUB problem

## 1. Fourier-family obstruction is rigorous
Jaming–Matolcsi–Móra–Szöllősi–Weiner proved that for every member of the two-parameter Fourier family F(a,b), the pair {I,F(a,b)} cannot be extended to a fourth mutually unbiased basis. This is a genuine whole-family theorem and is the cleanest external obstruction available for our intended route.

## 2. The older H2-reducible/Karlsson route is not currently safe
The 2021 H2-reducible theorem was used in later literature to constrain a hypothetical MUB quadruple. A 2025 Comment identified a mistake in a lemma from the earlier Chen–Yu work and stated that three later theorems derived from it are affected. A 2025 Reply was published, but it does not provide a new independently verified proof of the disputed general theorem sufficient to restore that route for our purposes.

Therefore the present project MUST NOT treat a continuum non-extendability theorem for the whole Dita/Karlsson sector as established solely from that H2-reducible argument.

## 3. The 2025 Hadamard-cube work gives the most promising structural bridge
Matolcsi–Matszangosz–Varga–Weiner characterize MUB triplets using Hadamard cubes. They conjecture that every dimension-6 MUB triplet is, up to permutational unitary equivalence, obtained from a construction whose three transition matrices consist of one matrix in the Szöllősi family X(α), one in the Fourier family F(a,b), and one in the transposed Fourier family F^T(a,b). They explicitly state that proving this conjecture would imply Zauner's conjecture that the maximum number of MUBs in C^6 is three.

The conjecture is not a theorem yet.

## 4. A complete order-six Hadamard classification now exists
Cárdenes Wuttig and Tindall (2026) report a complete exact finite-incidence classification of 6×6 complex Hadamard matrices. This removes the older uncertainty about the ambient Hadamard class space, but the classification itself does not settle the MUB problem because a MUB quartet imposes simultaneous compatibility conditions among several transition matrices.

## 5. Consequence for this project
The strongest valid target is now K_6^(3)-specific:

  prove that every genuine third-MUB component recovered from Karlsson's family has a transition matrix in the Fourier family (or establish the stronger X/F/F^T triplet structure).

Once a Fourier transition matrix is established inside a hypothetical quartet, the rigorous Jaming et al. theorem supplies the contradiction.

This is a structural theorem target, not yet a proved result of this project.
