# Literature used in the solution attack

- arXiv:0902.0882, Jaming, Matolcsi, Mora, Szöllősi, Weiner: proves that no MUB quartet can contain a pair {I,F(a,b)} for any Fourier-family parameters; also gives an infinite MUB-triplet construction and discusses Zauner's Dita-family construction.
- arXiv:2503.14752, Matolcsi, Matszangosz, Varga, Weiner: introduces Hadamard cubes and Conjectures 1 and 2. Conjecture 1 predicts a Fourier/X/transpose-Fourier structure for every MUB triplet in C^6; it is not proved.
- arXiv:2504.13067 and arXiv:2504.15576: 2025 Comment and Reply concerning the disputed H2-reducible theorem. This package treats the exact-nine-count exclusion route as invalid for present purposes.
- arXiv:2608.18053, Cárdenes Wuttig and Tindall: complete classification of order-six complex Hadamard matrices. This classifies the CHM ambient space but does not itself solve the MUB problem.
