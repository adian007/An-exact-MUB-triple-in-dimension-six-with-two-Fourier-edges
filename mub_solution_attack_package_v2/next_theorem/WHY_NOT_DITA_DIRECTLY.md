# Why we should not claim a Dita-continuum fourth-MUB obstruction yet

Our earlier numerical work on the Dita circle found complete third-MUB bases and seven previously certified fourth-vector obstructions at selected λ values. It also found a pool fold at λ* and an A4 orbit structure.

However, the old literature route that ruled out H2-reducible matrices in a hypothetical quartet was challenged in 2025 because a prerequisite lemma was identified as erroneous. Consequently, the old Dita-family continuum statement is not used as a proof here.

The robust route is instead:

  K3 third-MUB structure
      -> Fourier-family transition matrix
      -> rigorous whole-Fourier-family quartet obstruction.

This avoids relying on the disputed H2-reducible theorem.
