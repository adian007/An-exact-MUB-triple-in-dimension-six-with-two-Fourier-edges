# Phase 2: pi/3 B3 reconstruction probe

Date: 2026-09-30

## Scope

This is a bounded field-recognition diagnostic for the first recovered
pi/3 clique. It is not an algebraic reconstruction and does not upgrade any
numerical claim.

## Input

- Source: `results/campaigns/k3_fourier_structure/diagnostic_2026-09-28.json`
- Parameter: `lambda = pi/3`
- Clique: first stored diagnostic clique
- Arithmetic: stored `float64` components

## Result

Recognition against the real basis generated by
`Q(sqrt(2), sqrt(3))` recognized 70 of 72 real components within
`1e-12`. The largest componentwise discrepancy was approximately
`2.569e-12`; the mean discrepancy was approximately `9.619e-14`.

These numbers are consistent with a possible small-field description but do
not prove one. The source precision is insufficient for exact identity
testing, and the two unresolved components prevent even this bounded probe
from being treated as a complete representation.

## High-precision refinement

The first recovered clique was regenerated from the exact numerical Dita
formula using 100 decimal-digit mpmath refinement, with five unit-circle and
five independent MU equations per vector. All six vectors converged. The
independent checks were:

- maximum equation residual per vector: `1.143e-100`;
- maximum pairwise orthogonality residual: `1.446e-100`;
- maximum MU residual against all six columns of `H`: `1.714e-100`.

The resulting artifact is
`results/phase2_pi3_B3_mp100.json` with SHA-256
`4F8FD6CAAE5400ABA10AA071DC5C47D2731894ECE20AFEEFAB9CD2396EB8206C`.

A bounded PSLQ probe found clear low-degree relations for the repeated
10-degree trigonometric coordinates, while several other coordinates first
returned degree-nine relations with coefficients of order `1e10`. Those
relations are candidates only; they have not been independently certified as
minimal polynomials or shown to define a common coefficient field.

## Gate decision

**Phase 2 exact Gröbner elimination: not started.** The required exact B3
coefficients are unavailable. A floating-coefficient Gröbner calculation
would violate the certificate rules in
`docs/research/certificates/certificate_design.md`.

The phase templates for all three ±-pairs share the same polynomial `P(u)`.
Modulo `q^12-q^6+1`, the 15 pairwise inner products of the six-column
template vanish identically. Therefore the specialization `a=b=c=u` gives a
particularly small exact candidate B3 over the field defined by `P(u)` and
the q-linear relation in `results/pi3_phase_groebner_basis.txt`.

The corrected corresponding 17-equation W1 export is
`symbolic_export/w1_pi3_exact_abc.m2`. The earlier
`w1_pi3_exact_ansatz.m2` used the invalid shortcut `a=b=c` and is retained
only as deprecated exploratory output.

The Macaulay2 execution was attempted twice. The normal run was denied access
to the Docker configuration; the elevated run reached Docker but failed
because the Docker Desktop Linux engine pipe was not present. No Gröbner
verdict was recorded, and no exact W1 claim is made.

The exact candidate's transition (H^\dagger B_3) also fits the two-parameter
Fourier family numerically after the recorded row/column permutations, with
maximum residual `4.97e-16`. The symbolic cross-ratio upgrade was attempted
but remains computationally unfinished; this fit is therefore still labeled
NUMERICAL and does not invoke the published Fourier obstruction theorem.
